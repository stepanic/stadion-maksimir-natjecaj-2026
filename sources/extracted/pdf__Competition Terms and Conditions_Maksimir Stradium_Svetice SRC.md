1.4 PROFESSIONAL FIELD, TYPE, PURPOSE AND STAGE OF THE COMPETITION
• COMPETITON PROCEDURE TYPE
The competition shall be conducted as an open competition.
• PROFESSIONAL FIELD
The competition is an architectural and urban design competition.
• PURPOSE OF THE COMPETITION
The competition is both an ideas competition and a project competition.
The purpose of the idea’s competition is to obtain design proposals in order to examine
possible approaches to construction or spatial organisation without commissioning
design services or establishing any other exploitation rights over the architectural work
submitted. The ideas competition covers Svetice Sports and Recreation Centre,
including the athletics-recreation complex and indoor sports complex, and Borongaj
Transit Hub area.
The purpose of the project competition is the commissioning of design services.
The project competition area relates to Maksimir Football Stadium Complex including the
stadium building, auxiliary pitches with associated facilities and the related infrastructure
and landscape areas.
• NUMBER OF COMPETITION STAGES
The competition shall be conducted in a single stage.
1.5 OBJECTIVE OF THE COMPETITION
The objective of the competition is the selection of the best architectural and urban
design proposal for Maksimir Stadium Complex, Sports and Recreation Centre and
Borongaj Transit Hub, that most successfully meets the needs of the Contracting
Authority in accordance with the assessment criteria.

1.6 LIST OF MEMBERS OF THE COMPETITION BODIES
The appointment of members of the competition bodies has been carried out in
accordance with the Regulations, as follows:
A. Nine (9) members of the Jury and their alternates have been appointed in
compliance with the following requirements:
- the majority of Jury members are professionals in the field of architecture and
urban planning, namely architects, registered architects and/or registered urban
planners
- at least one third of the Jury members possess qualifications identical or equivalent
to those required for competitors
- two thirds of the Jury members are independent of the Contracting Authority
- alternate Jury members are professionals in the field of architecture and urban
planning and are to attend all Jury sessions, with voting rights only when officially
replacing an excused voting member of the Jury
- in accordance with the official interpretation of the Regulations by the Croatian
Chamber of Architects and the Croatian Architects’ Association, the Alternate Jury
Member representing the Government of the Republic of Croatia and the Alternate
Jury Member representing the City of Zagreb may not participate in Jury
deliberations simultaneously, but only individually when replacing the respective
appointed Jury member.
B. Twelve (12) Professional Advisers have been appointed in compliance with the
Regulations.
These are recognised specialists in fields related to the subject of the competition.
They may be engaged by the Contracting Authority, at the request of the Jury, to
provide written opinions on the competition documentation and/or on submitted
competition entries and may participate in Jury sessions upon invitation.
C. Three (3) members of the Technical Committee have been appointed.
All members of the Technical Committee are registered architects.
D. The Secretary and the Deputy Secretary of the Competition have also been
appointed.
Both are registered architects.
